# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). The version is `udg_catalogue.__version__`, and the run manifest records it next to the sci-etl-core version. Catalogue runs published before 1.0.0 predate versioning, so their manifests carry no catalogue version.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Polite arXiv limiter** (`udg_catalogue.arxiv.PoliteRateLimiter`, `arxiv` config section). One arXiv request at a time, each starting `min_interval` plus up to `jitter` seconds (3 + 0–2 s) after the previous one ended, listing and full text alike. An `httpx` response hook reports a 429 or 503, which pauses every arXiv request for `cooldown` seconds (60), doubled for each further one up to `max_cooldown` (900), or longer when `Retry-After` asks. It replaces `full_text.max_concurrency: 4`, whose four parallel downloads got the 2026-10-21 run throttled for about 30 minutes. `http.max_retries` is 5, so a request can wait out about 15 minutes of throttling, and `pipeline.search_delay` is 0, since the limiter paces listing requests too.
- **Full-text cache** (`udg_catalogue.arxiv.FullTextCache`, `paths.full_text_cache`). Parsed full text is stored per versioned arXiv id under `data/full_text/` and read back instead of downloaded, so `--reprocess` and rebuilds after a prompt change need no arXiv downloads. An abstract fallback is not cached. Reprocessing 5 papers took 89 s with downloads and 50 s from the cache, most of it paging the listing.
- **Alias and namesake matching.** A leading `UDG` label before a designation is dropped (`UDG-DF44` matches `Dragonfly 44`). `AliasKeyStep` joins a name with aliases in parentheses to rows named by them (`Yagi276 (DF28)` and `DF28`). `NamesakeSplitStep` splits rows that share a name key but lie more than `deduplication.namesake_separation_arcmin` (10′) apart into separate galaxies, and places rows without a position by distance (`deduplication.namesake_distance_ratio`, 1.5). The 2026-10-21 data had 30 such keys: `UDG 1` to `UDG 23`, each merging a Hydra I UDG with an unrelated field UDG, and the Dragonfly names `DF1`, `DF2`, `DF4` and `DF7` merging Coma galaxies with NGC 1052 group galaxies, so the Coma DF2 carried NGC 1052-DF2's 22.1 Mpc. The catalogue grows from 977 to 1,012 galaxies.

### Changed

- **Papers in flight.** `pipeline.max_concurrency` is 16, up from 6. arXiv requests are paced by the limiter regardless, so the extra papers are DeepSeek relevance and extraction calls running alongside the downloads.
- **Search query.** `pipeline.search_query` quotes the phrase "ultra-diffuse", since arXiv split the unquoted `abs:ultra-diffuse` into two words and matched 166 unrelated papers (diffuse radio emission, interstellar ices). It also matches "ultradiffuse", "UDG(s)", "SMUDGes", and NGC 1052-DF2, NGC 1052-DF4 and Dragonfly 44 by name, in titles as well as abstracts and across all of astro-ph instead of astro-ph.GA alone. It matched 462 listings on 2026-10-21, 70 of them missed before, including the Coma discovery paper (1410.8141) and the TRGB distances to NGC 1052-DF2 and DF4. `pipeline.total_limit` is 2000, so the limit no longer stops a full run.
- **Relevance prompt.** A paper is relevant when it measures specific observed UDGs or UDG candidates, from new or re-analysed observations. It is not relevant when it is a simulation, only fits gravity or dark matter models to published values, mentions UDGs only in passing, or is a review, white paper, forecast or instrument description without UDG measurements. Relevance answers cached under the old prompt are not reused.
- **Catalogue rebuilt from scratch (2026-10-21).** 462 submissions screened, 323 read in full, 1,012 galaxies (977 before the namesake split above), each with its `source_papers`, and `data/run_manifest.json`. It replaces the 1,927-galaxy catalogue of 2026-09-24, built before extraction was limited to the objects a paper classifies as UDGs and before sky matching merged duplicates. It passes the published-catalogue checks for sky duplicates, paper-local names and the extraction rules, so their expected-failure markers are removed; the check for dark-matter fractions of exactly 0 or 1 still fails on 8 objects.
- **Cascading relevance filter on.** `config.yaml` enables the embedding prescreen ahead of the LLM check, at threshold 0.35, with three more concepts: globular clusters and dark matter of UDGs, HI in gas-rich UDGs, and distances to NGC 1052-DF2 and DF4.

- **Board layout.** The dashboard is laid out as a classic bulletin board in the same slate colours: a ruled site header with the catalogue size, a nav strip with a link per page and the time, a breadcrumb, a board index listing the selection's eight largest constellations and clusters with their galaxies, confirmed and mapped counts and nearest mapped galaxy, every section in a ruled board under an ink category bar, the sidebar's navigation, filters and facets as boards of their own, paper search hits as striped topic rows, and on every page a statistics box (quality flags, mapped count and median distance, the largest and most massive galaxy, the last build) with a legend, and a footer with the versions. Type is Verdana, corners are square, and Inter is no longer loaded.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

First versioned release, on the released sci-etl-core 0.7.0.

### Added

- **Paper search page.** `app.py` shows four regions on one screen: a query bar whose parsed `DiscoveryResult.chips` are drawn as chips (active facet filters as dashed chips), `total_matched` and `elapsed_ms` in mono, the hit list with the keyword and meaning ranks and the fused score of each hit, a Plotly graph canvas, and an inspector. Facets sit in the sidebar as `value · count` rows and re-query on click. `?view=`, `?q=` and `?paper=` query parameters deep-link to a view, a query and a selected paper.
- **Persistent banner for weakened searches.** `degraded` and `skipped` legs of a `DiscoveryResult`, and a discovery graph whose communities did not converge, are named in a banner that stays while the condition holds, never a toast.
- **Graph canvas controls.** Nodes are filled by community with the Okabe-Ito categorical palette (six hues, a seventh community and beyond in grey) and sized by degree; the seed is the one accent element; the current hits stay at full ink and the rest are dimmed; every edge `kind` present gets a checkbox; a node is selected by clicking it or through a selectbox; a toggle makes the canvas fullscreen. The layout is seeded from the record ids, so the same graph renders the same way twice.
- **Inspector.** For the selected paper: id, title, authors, year and categories; the catalogue rows whose `source_papers` name it, with the measured columns; its rows of `data/rejected_galaxies.db`; and the run manifest's model, prompt hashes, library version and build date as the extraction stamp. `Open in graph` recenters the discovery graph on it.
- **`udg_catalogue.ui` package** of pure functions (tokens, theme CSS, chips, facets, graph canvas, hit rows, inspector rows, charts) with no Streamlit import, covered by `tests/test_ui_*.py` and included in the 100% coverage gate.
- **Slate theme.** `.streamlit/config.toml` carries the palette (`#F4F5F7` canvas, `#334155` type and borders, `#6366F1` accent), Inter and JetBrains Mono with system fallbacks, 1 px borders and 2 px corners; `udg_catalogue/ui/theme.py` adds the CSS the theme cannot express. `browser.gatherUsageStats` is off.
- **`docs/interface.md`** with the page layout, what each region binds to, what the Streamlit version does not do, and the theme tokens.
- **`python main.py --reprocess ARXIV_ID ...`** forgets the given papers on the run's state manager (`forget`, new in sci-etl-core 0.7) and pages the listing from its first entry, so a corrected prompt or validator re-extracts chosen papers. The log names the ids forgotten and the ids that were never processed. With `--index-papers` the ids are forgotten in the indexing state instead. The CSV exporter and the paper memory replace a paper's earlier rows and passages, and cached LLM answers are reused when the prompt and model are unchanged.
- **Embedding prescreen** (`prescreen.enabled`, off by default): an `AsyncEmbeddingRelevanceFilter` over the configured embeddings provider runs before the DeepSeek relevance check through `AsyncCascadingRelevanceFilter`, and rejects a paper whose title and abstract score below `prescreen.threshold` against every `prescreen.concepts` entry without an LLM call. An active prescreen is logged as a warning because it changes what the run screens and costs. It requires `embeddings.enabled`.
- **Text index tuning** (`search_index.cache_size_mb`, default 64, and `search_index.mmap_size_mb`, default none) passed to the FTS5 store as `SqliteTuning`.
- **Contract suites** under `tests/contract/`: sci-etl-core's `ExtractorContract` runs against `LoggingExtractor` over the arXiv extractor, and `ExporterContract` against the raw-catalogue CSV exporter. `requirements/dev.txt` installs the `testing` extra.
- **Package version** `udg_catalogue.__version__`, recorded in the run manifest as `software.udg-catalogue`, where earlier manifests recorded `null`.

### Changed

- **sci-etl-core 0.7** is required: `sci-etl-core[...]>=0.7.0,<0.8` in `requirements.txt`, `requirements/dev.txt` and `requirements/local.txt`, and the suite runs with `-W error::DeprecationWarning`.
- **Paper search's "papers contain every keyword" figure** comes from `count_matches`, which counts in the index, instead of loading every matching id.
- **Hybrid search draws 500 candidates per leg** instead of 100, following the sci-etl-core 0.7 default; set `search.hybrid.candidate_pool: 100` in `config.yaml` to keep the old pool.
- **Continuous integration** installs sci-etl-core from PyPI instead of its `master` branch and fails on any `DeprecationWarning`.
- **Chart colours chosen for reading, chrome colours for the page.** Clusters and graph communities use the Okabe-Ito colour-blind-safe order; dark-matter fraction and completeness use Viridis, and a galaxy without the measure is drawn grey under a `no value` legend entry instead of as zero; by cluster, the six largest clusters get a hue each and the rest are grey; axes, grids and type use the slate tokens. The analytics plots are now Plotly figures drawn by `udg_catalogue.ui.charts` with log-spaced bins and the median marked in the accent colour (the PNG report figures of `main.py` are unchanged).
- **Empty states are sentences, not alerts.** A missing catalogue, a missing paper index, an empty selection and the empty inspector sections each show one line in the secondary colour. A missing paper index disables the search page only; the table, map and analytics pages keep working from the committed CSV.
- **Paper search labels.** `Display Mode:` is `View`; the related-papers button is `Select` on each hit; the graph table under the old chart is replaced by the inspector.
